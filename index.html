Father of Ultra

Ultra Ken • Supreme Commander of the Inter Galactic Defense Force

The Legendary Hero of M78

Father of Ultra, whose real name is Ken, is a veteran hero and the supreme commander of the Inter Galactic Defense Force on Planet 47 in the Land of Light (Nebula M78).

He is an ancient and revered figure who led the Ultra Warriors to victory during the Great Ultimate Galactic War against Emperor Empera. Recognizable by his massive "Ultimate Horns" and signature beard, he stands as a symbol of justice, wisdom, and absolute authority.

Signature Abilities & Weapons

Father Shot (Father Beam)

His primary finishing move, performed by forming an L-shape with his arms, releasing a devastating beam of energy charged with supreme power.

Crescent Shot

A blade-like energy beam fired from his hand that can slice through enemy forces or destroy powerful monsters.

Ultra Array

A specialized light technique emitted from his hands, capable of stunning, paralyzing, or dismantling dark energy fields.

Ultimate Key & Feather

Wields legendary relics including the Ultra Feather and the Feather Sword, capable of turning the tide in apocalyptic battles.

Historical Significance

During the Great Ultra War millions of years ago, Ken awakened the power of the Ultimate Horns and defeated the Alien Empera, saving the Land of Light from total destruction. Following the victory, he established the Inter Galactic Defense Force to maintain peace throughout the universe, mentoring generations of Ultra Heroes including the Ultra Brothers.
